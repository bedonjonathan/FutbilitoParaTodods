The models

Your sheet (season home/away)
A straight port of the Goals tab: home attack = home GF/GP ÷ league avg, away defence = away GA/GP ÷ league avg, projected goals = attack × defence × league average, then a Poisson score grid.
Last-5 form (+xG)
Same idea, but strengths come from each team's last 5 matches, with goals blended 50/50 with xG when available, and shrunk toward average because 5 games is a small sample.
Dixon-Coles (time-weighted)
Fits attack, defence and home advantage for every team at once by maximum likelihood over 3+ seasons, weighting recent games more (half-life ≈ 1 year). Adjusts schedule strength automatically and corrects 0-0 / 1-1 frequencies.
ML Poisson (form + Elo)
Machine learning: Poisson regressions trained on thousands of past matches. Inputs are each side's last-5 goals, shots on target, points and the Elo gap. The model learns how much each signal is really worth.
Blend
Geometric mean of the models' expected goals. Ensembles are usually more reliable than any single model.

Fixes vs the spreadsheet

  • Home Wins % (B9) summed H40:N40, which skips the 1-0 cell (G40), so home-win chances were understated.
  • Under 3.5 (B18) omitted the 0-3 scoreline (F43).
  • American odds used (1/p − 1) × 100, which is only correct for underdogs. Favourites are now negative (e.g. 70% → −233).
  • Correct-score labels like “1-1”, “1-2” had been auto-converted by Excel into dates (2023-01-01…).
  • The 20-branch IFS lookups are replaced by automatic lookups, so new or promoted teams just work.
